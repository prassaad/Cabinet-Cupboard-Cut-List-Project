No module selected. Click + New above to add one, then configure its sizes, back panel, sheet stock and banding here.

One-time project setup. These settings rarely change while you're designing — set them once and they apply everywhere.

Sizes (mm)

Back panel (mm)

Overlay = full panel on the rear. Groove/rabbet = inset panel housed in the carcass; depth = how far it sits into the groove, setback = how far it's recessed from the back.

Top & bottom panels (mm)

Inset = panel sits between the sides (sides run full height); Outset = panel caps over the sides at full width (sides shorten by the panel thickness). Depth is independent of the sides — it may be shallower or deeper than the cabinet; anchor places it against the back, front or centre. Defaults match the sides. Cut list updates automatically.

Sheet stock

Defines the panel size and blade width used to nest parts in the Sheet layout tab.

Edge banding L=length · W=width edges

Tick the edges to band per part — edges are named as on the part (Front/Back are the long visible edges; the ends butt into the carcass). Thickness is subtracted from the raw cut size on those edges (finished size fills the opening); width is the roll to buy — keep it ≥ the material thickness so it covers the edge.
